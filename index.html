RACE BRIEFING / MIDNIGHT CIRCUIT

Build one unbroken line.

Finish three long laps against three rivals. Position matters, but a clean drift score decides who truly owns the circuit.

Steer with A / D. Hold SHIFT while turning to initiate a drift; tap SPACE for a sharper handbrake rotation. Countersteer to hold angle. Release and drive clean to bank the chain.

Cross the lit apex zones while sliding to raise your multiplier. A wall hit breaks the chain. On mobile, auto-acceleration is enabled.
